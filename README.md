### What I build

🤖 **LLMs, end to end.** Fine-tune, evaluate, and serve small models, not just call an API.

🔎 **Retrieval that's honest.** RAG that cites its sources and flags what it does not know, instead of bluffing.

🔌 **Agents and automation.** MCP integrations, Django REST, and the glue that makes tools, CRMs, and browsers act, not just reply.
